05 What is missing

Twelve gaps no document covers

These are what separate a trusted agency from a clever funnel. Most cost time, not money.

1 · Proof assets

No consented student stories, letters or room tours yet. Target: 3 by 15 December, with names and numbers hidden and permission in writing. NM

2 · A parent path

The parent often pays. Needed: a parent page, a 90-second parent video, and a Facebook FAQ: costs, safety, who is accountable, what we will not promise. NM CLAUDE

3 · Privacy and consent

We hold passports and grades. Needed: a short privacy notice (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, Türkiye's KVKK), a retention rule, and an opt-in tick for WhatsApp on the assessment and application. CLAUDE drafts, a lawyer checks.

4 · An anti-scam “How to verify us” page

Students fear fake agents. One page: we never ask for tuition, we only take payment on myorbuni.com / Whop / Paystack, our team with photos, our Istanbul address, and our WhatsApp number with the blue tick once verified.

5 · Tracking before ads

Meta pixel + Conversions API, one tracking link per channel and creator, and video start / 50% / finish events. Without these, any ad money is spent blind.

6 · University deposit deadlines

Each offer page should show the university's own deadline (usually 1–2 weeks). Send me the list per university. NM

7 · A referral programme for students

Every placed student knows 5 more. Pay a fixed thank-you (for example $50) only after the friend pays the service fee. Whop affiliates can track it.

8 · A reply rota

Who answers when, in Istanbul and Lagos time, including weekends. The AI covers nights; a person covers high-intent leads in working hours. GW

9 · Reviews

A Google Business Profile, and a review request 7 days after arrival. Reviews are free proof that also helps search ranking.

10 · Intake calendar

When applications open and close at each partner university, for both spring and fall intakes, so content and ads run in the right weeks. Confirm per university [CHECK].

11 · Complaint and refund procedure

Who answers, within how many hours, how the refund tiers (10% / 20% / 30–40%) are explained, and how it is logged. This also protects the Whop and Paystack accounts.

12 · Maximum cost per student

A hard rule before ads: one paid student may cost at most about 25% of the average fee (about $300). Above that, stop and fix the funnel.

What the “Accelerator” tiers suggested, and what we do instead

“Tier 3 VIP $3,000+”

Premier at $2,650 already fills this place. A higher tier needs vendors we have not tested yet (a licensed visa agent in each country, luxury transport). Revisit in June 2027, once two Premier students have been delivered well.

“Quad revenue”

University and housing commissions are normal in this industry. Rule: written contract first, disclosed to the student, never counted in forecasts before the money arrives. The fee stays the main income.

“$250–$800 consultation”

Too high before we have proof. Instead: a $25–$49 Profile Review on Whop Calendar Bookings, 30 minutes, credited in full against Plus or Premier if they sign within 90 days.

Budget rules

  • Start: $10–$15 a day, one country, 4–6 ads, for 7 days
  • Goal event: assessment finished (not page view, not “lead”)
  • Kill rule: an ad that spends 2× the target cost per finished assessment with none, off
  • Target to begin with: about $8 per finished assessment (assumption; reset after 50)

Creative: what to make

  • 70% image ads, 30% video. One hour a week makes 5–10 new images from the best lines of the main video.

Meta personal-attributes rule: an ad may not say or imply it knows the viewer's nationality, race, religion, finances and so on. “Are you a Nigerian student?” or “You can't afford the UK?” will be rejected. “For students in Nigeria…” and “Studying abroad on a budget?” are fine.
