What I fixed

  • Fixed

    Feed view counts did not move while people were reading

    The counts in the database were correct (your three posts have 4, 3 and 3 views, matching the real records). But the Feed was never switched on for live updates, so a number only changed when someone reloaded the page. Your own view also didn't show until a reload. Now views, likes and comments update on every open screen as they happen. A new or deleted post refreshes the list unless someone is typing.

  • Fixed

    "Lead overdue" alerts were never sent

    The portal had a "lead overdue" alert, but nothing ever raised it, so leads went quiet without anyone hearing about it. Now an hourly check (09:00–20:00 Istanbul) alerts you and the lead's owner, once a day per lead, or with one summary when more than 5 are waiting. The first run sent 4 alerts.

  • Fixed

    Finance dashboard was not live

    "Money at a glance" is built to refresh when a payment lands, but its table was also never switched on for live updates. It now refreshes by itself. People still only receive rows they are allowed to see.

  • Fixed

    WhatsApp follow-ups ran on an older version

    The automatic WhatsApp follow-up job still sent team warnings, such as low Twilio balance, by email only. It now uses the portal alerts (bell, phone push and email). Test run: OK, balance $19.98.

  • Fixed

    Your test chat was stuck on "needs a person"

    Because of the earlier refund question, the AI stayed paused on your own number. I pressed "Done" for you, so the AI answers you again.

  • Fixed

    AI replies used formatting WhatsApp can't show

    In testing, the AI wrote **bold**, which WhatsApp shows as stars. It now uses WhatsApp's own *bold* and plain links.
