Payment structure

What Orbuni charges, and when

Applying is free. You pay in three clearly separate places, and Orbuni's fee is the only one that comes to us.

Apply — freeNo fee to apply and no payment before a university sends you an offer letter.
Our service fee — once, after your offerPick Standard, Plus or Premier. Same price whichever university you choose.
Tuition — direct to the universityYou pay the university itself, never Orbuni. Government fees (visa, residence permit) are also paid by you directly.

Standard

$800 once

≈ $67 a month if spread over a year

  • University and programme selection
  • Application preparation and submission
  • Document checking before anything is sent
  • Every update, from submission to offer letter
  • A named Orbuni counsellor
Most chosen

Plus

$1,650 once

≈ $138 a month if spread over a year

  • Everything in Standard
  • Visa assistance — what to prepare and how to apply
  • Airport pickup when you land in Türkiye
  • Arrival assistance in your first days

Premier

$2,650 or 2 × $1,325

Second part taken 30 days later, then it stops

  • Everything in Plus
  • A trusted visa agent in your own country
  • Escort to your university for registration
  • Residence permit application support

The monthly figures are only a budgeting guide — you pay the service fee once, not monthly. If a service cannot be completed, part of the fee comes back: 10% on Standard, 20% on Plus, 30–40% on Premier.
