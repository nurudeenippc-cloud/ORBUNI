## Posting tips
- Post the carousel first, the launch film 24–48 hours later (or the same evening as a Reel).
- Best times for Nigeria/Ghana audiences: 7–9 pm WAT, Tue–Thu.
- Pin the launch Reel to the top of the profile for launch week.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour — it lifts reach.
- For ads: use the 4:5 film in Feed, the 9:16 film in Reels/Stories, CTA "Learn more" → myorbuni.com/#start
